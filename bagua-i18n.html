📜 哲學導論（中 / EN 對照）

中文

幾千年前，古人觀察天地，把世界歸納成八種基本能量——天、澤、火、雷、風、水、山、地，寫成《易經》的八卦。從陰（- -）與陽（—）三爻相疊而成。太極生兩儀，兩儀生四象，四象生八卦。兩卦相疊成六十四卦。八卦也像一家人：乾父、坤母與六個子女。在這套系統裡，我們把它重新詮釋成八種內在能量，讓古人的智慧成為認識自己的鏡子。

English (draft)

Thousands of years ago, observers of nature in ancient China distilled the world into eight basic energies — Heaven, Lake, Fire, Thunder, Wind, Water, Mountain, Earth — written as the Eight Trigrams (Bagua) of the I Ching. They are built from just Yin (a broken line) and Yang (a solid line), stacked three high: Taiji gives rise to the two forms, the two forms to the four images, the four images to the eight trigrams. Stack two trigrams and you get a hexagram — the 64 of them describe life's situations. The trigrams are also a family: Qián the father, Kūn the mother, and their six children. Here we reinterpret them as eight inner energies — so this ancient wisdom becomes a mirror for knowing yourself.

A mirror for understanding yourself and your relationships — not fortune-telling, not destiny, not a compatibility score. These energies flow and can grow; your result is a tendency, not a fixed label. Everything here comes from your own self-reflection — a starting point, not a verdict.
