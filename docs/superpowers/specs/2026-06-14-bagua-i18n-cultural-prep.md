## 0. 翻譯原則（文化性介紹）
1. **保留羅馬拼音＋簡短意譯並陳**：第一次出現用「Qián (乾) — The Creative / Heaven」，之後可只用 Qián 或英文。讓讀者學到原詞，又懂意思。
2. **尊重來源、不神祕化、不誇大**：說明它是「古人觀察自然的歸納」「一套描述能量的語言」；**沿用既有誠實框架**——是反思的鏡子，不是算命/命定/性向評測。
3. **卦象符號（☰☱…）與卦名不翻**：符號全球通用；卦名保留拼音。
4. **用既有的功能化詮釋當橋樑**：我們把八卦重新詮釋成「八種內在能量」，這對外國讀者其實**比傳統卦辭更好懂**——主打這個。
5. **trigram vs hexagram 要分清**：八卦＝trigram（三爻）；六十四卦＝hexagram（六爻）。
6. **五行用 "Five Phases"（學術較準）或 "Five Elements"（通俗）**：建議首次 "Wu Xing (Five Phases)"，內文用 Five Elements 較親民。

## 4. 哲學導論 EN 草稿（「八卦是什麼」門面/認識八卦）
- Thousands of years ago, observers of nature in ancient China distilled the world into **eight basic energies** — Heaven, Lake, Fire, Thunder, Wind, Water, Mountain, Earth — written as the **Eight Trigrams (Bagua)** of the I Ching.
- They are built from just two marks: **Yin** (a broken line) and **Yang** (a solid line), stacked three high. *Taiji* gives rise to the two forms, the two forms to the four images, and the four images to the eight trigrams.
- Stack two trigrams and you get a **hexagram** — the 64 hexagrams describe the situations of life.
- The eight trigrams are also a **family**: Qián the father, Kūn the mother, and their six children.
- In this system we reinterpret the trigrams as **eight inner energies** — so this ancient wisdom becomes a mirror for knowing yourself.
